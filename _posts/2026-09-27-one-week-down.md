I have survived my first week of studying! It has been a range of things,
- exciting
- anxiety-enducing
- tiring
- confusing
- interesting
- inspiring
- career-affirming

I am taking two modules this term, _Information Seeking and Use_ (ISU), and _Knowledge Organisation_ (KO). There are definite links between the two modules, however they are very different.

<!--more-->

I have two study days and am intending to stick with a schedule, one module a day, to get into a good routine. This week, I spent Thursday working on ISU and Friday on KO. There are also live sessions each week for both modules, on Tuesdays (ISU) and Thursdays (KO), so I am treating those as my introduction each week before digging into the main work on my scheduled days. If I am not able to attend the live sessions, I will watch the recording first thing in the morning on my assigned day.

Thursday was exhausting (probably not helped by Wednesday night's pub quiz, meaning a late night and a couple of beers!) as this module is a **lot** of reading - I have module notes to read, and lots of recommended papers for additional information. The content in the first week was interesting, and all made sense: it is about how individuals search for information, and how their personalities and contexts will have an effect on their information seeking behaviour. However, my eyes and brain were very tired after a full day of taking in information, even with some breaks to post about the suggested discussion topics in the forum.

Friday, in contrast, was exhilarating: it turns out that I love knowledge organisation! We started with an overview of the goals of knowledge organisation:
> "To organise knowledge is to gather together what we know into a comprehensive organised structure, to show its parts and their relationships." [(Vickery, 2008)](https://web.archive.org/web/20100125050134/http://www.lucis.me.uk/knowlorg.htm#start)

We will be covering indexing and classification, and working on a scenario based assignment throughout the term; this suits my learning style as I enjoy putting things into practice. I was less tired by the end of the day, and had to force myself to stop working on the domain analysis for my proposed scenario at dinner time!

I have spent the weekend doing a few things in preperation for next week's studying:
- Downloading as many of the recommended papers as I can - not all are accessible, some I can find via the university's database, some I need to do more searching for, so doing this now means that I can concentrate on reading them next week
- Charging my old Kindle and transferring the PDFs onto it - I hope the e-ink screen will be easier on my eyes.
- Reading about domain analysis and documentation - I am definitely inspired by the KO module and excited to learn more!
- Saving other interesting papers and reports to read - I went down a few rabbit holes in my KO reading. Some link with the ISU module, and it was good to see how the work connects up. Other papers are not relevant right now, but look interesting, so I'm saving them to read later.

All in all, it's been a very interesting week, and I am excited to see where both modules take me in the coming weeks.
