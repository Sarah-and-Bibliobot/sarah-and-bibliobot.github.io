It has been a lot of years since I last formally studied, or had to write reports and assignments. I have written evaluation reports, grant applications and many many student worksheets and lesson plans, but my style is very much non-academic!

To help me get started before I need to submit my first assignment for my MSc, I have taken a look at some of the study skills books available in my library, so that when I need some help, I know which ones to borrow.

In the end, I borrowed two books on academic writing - one a readable general overview to get some inspiration and give me things to think about when I start, the second a very short guide with more specific examples. Some of the other more comprehensive guides looked good, but will be more useful later once I'm writing my first reports and assignments, so I have earmarked them to reconsider later on in the term.

I also borrowed one on critical reading, as I haven't needed to read research papers before so want to make sure that I'm doing it efficiently. Some other critical thinking skills guides looked useful, but more comprehensive, so I'll see how I get on for the first few weeks and then see where I need extra guidance.
